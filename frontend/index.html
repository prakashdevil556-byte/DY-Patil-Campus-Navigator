    About Campus

    Dr. D. Y. Patil Educational Complex

    Located in Sector 29, Nigdi Pradhikaran, Akurdi, Pune, the Dr. D. Y. Patil Educational Complex is a premier multidisciplinary campus spanning approximately 33 acres. It houses a wide range of institutions including engineering, pharmacy, architecture, management, schools, junior colleges, and a private international university.

    The campus is well-connected by road and public transport, situated along the old Mumbai-Pune highway with easy access from the Akurdi railway station and major bus routes.

    Our mission is to provide a centralized digital navigation platform for students, faculty, staff, and visitors to easily locate any institution, department, lab, or facility within the complex.
